What this covers

Balar FieldLog is an internal tool used by BALAR Associates, Inc. staff to record daily construction inspection observations and route field reports for engineer review. It is not offered to the public.

Google account access

The FieldLog server component connects to Google services (Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Apps Script email) using a single BALAR owned Google account. That access is used only to read and write BALAR's own FieldLog spreadsheet and Drive folders, and to send report notifications to BALAR staff. No other user signs in with Google, and no data from any other Google account is accessed.

Information collected

FieldLog stores the inspection information that BALAR inspectors enter: project details, dates, work descriptions, weather, equipment and workforce counts, site photographs, and location coordinates where an inspector records them. This information is kept in BALAR's Google Drive and Google Sheets and on BALAR's office server.

Use and sharing

Information is used only to prepare, review, and file BALAR's field inspection reports. It is not sold, and it is not shared outside BALAR except as part of reports delivered to the clients for whom the work is performed.
